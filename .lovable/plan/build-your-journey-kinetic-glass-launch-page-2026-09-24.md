# Build Your Journey — Kinetic Glass launch page

## Build
- Replace the blank home page with a dark, edge-to-edge cinematic launch experience titled **Build Your Journey**.
- Use oversized editorial typography, electric cyan/lime/coral accents, sparse glass overlays and a strong animated path motif.
- Keep the hero copy and both primary routes exactly aligned with the brief.

## Page story
- Add the proof strip, goal-to-guided-route experience and an interactive Life Map.
- Keep journeys separate by default; the visible “Connect with permission” control activates and deactivates the connecting paths.
- Add emotionally distinct, video-ready Football, Health and Law showcase bands.
- Add the Journey Studio builder route, the existing-product partner route and the two-choice closing section.

## Quality and trust
- Use concise British English and make private-by-default, reversible consent and user-controlled data explicit.
- Avoid unsupported claims and state that Health and Law journeys support — never replace — qualified professionals.
- Make every call-to-action scroll to its relevant section.
- Add responsive desktop/mobile layouts, restrained reveals, reduced-motion support, a custom favicon and complete page metadata.

## Technical details
- Keep all colour and typography values in the shared design tokens.
- Build media areas as native `<video>` elements with poster artwork, ready for later MP4/WebM source files.
- Verify the final page visually at desktop and mobile sizes and check the preview for errors.
