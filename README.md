# Build Your Journey

Create a completely new, premium, high-energy landing page for BUILD YOUR JOURNEY — not a career planner and not a generic goal tracker.

PRODUCT TRUTH

Build Your Journey is a platform ecosystem. A person or family can enter a goal, problem or life event and discover or create an end-to-end AI-led journey. Different journeys can connect through a permission-controlled Life Map, but ONLY when the user explicitly chooses. The platform also gives people and organisations with a similar specialist idea the infrastructure to build a Journey product, and gives existing AI-led products a trusted homepage/discovery and integration layer.

INITIAL PROOF PRODUCTS

Use these as vivid examples, not as the limits of the platform:

• The Journey Football — navigating opportunities, development, wellbeing, safeguarding and the wider family experience.

• The Journey Health — turning a confusing health situation into a clear, safe sequence of questions, evidence and next steps.

• The Journey Law — organising a legal problem into a guided route, documents, deadlines and appropriate professional support.

• Complaints — building a structured evidence-led escalation journey.

AUDIENCES AND PRIMARY ROUTES

1. People and families: “Find or build my journey”.

2. Builders and partners: “Bring a Journey product to the platform”.

The partner route must clearly cover both creating a new product with Journey Studio and connecting an existing AI-led product.

VISUAL DIRECTION

Make this feel like a buzzy product launch from an ambitious new technology platform: cinematic, kinetic, confident and emotionally human. Dark midnight/navy foundation with electric cyan, vivid lime and hot coral accents. Bold editorial typography, oversized statements, premium motion, glassy overlays used sparingly, and a distinctive animated path/network motif. Avoid beige, corporate HR visuals, generic SaaS dashboard cards, cartoon illustrations, and stock-office imagery.

FIRST VIEWPORT

Edge-to-edge cinematic hero with a large video-ready media layer and readable dark overlay. Headline: “Every goal starts a journey.” Supporting line: “Discover, build and connect AI-led journeys across the moments that shape your life — sharing between them only when you choose.” Primary CTA: “Explore journeys”. Secondary CTA: “Build on the platform”. Include an elegant visual Life Map showing Football, Health, Law and Complaints as separate glowing nodes. Connections should visually activate only when a visible “Connect with permission” control is switched on.

PAGE STORY

• Fast proof strip featuring Football, Health, Law and Complaints.

• An exciting “Start with what matters now” section showing how a user enters a goal and receives a guided route.

• Interactive Life Map section explaining that journeys stay separate by default and connect only with explicit permission.

• Cinematic showcase sections for Football, Health and Law, each designed to accept a short Runway video clip later. Include polished video placeholders now and ensure future MP4/WebM replacement is straightforward.

• “For builders” section explaining Journey Studio: shared AI intake, journey logic, milestones, evidence, permissions and family relationships.

• “Already built something brilliant?” section for existing AI-led products to become discoverable and optionally connect to the ecosystem.

• Strong closing CTA with the two user routes.

COPY AND TRUST

Use crisp British English. Keep copy specific and concise. Do not invent user counts, customer logos, testimonials, awards or pricing. Do not imply that AI replaces doctors or lawyers. Make consent central: private by default, explicit permission, reversible connections, user-controlled data.

INTERACTION AND QUALITY

Build responsive desktop and mobile experiences. Use smooth but restrained reveal animations, hover depth and animated connecting lines. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Buttons must work as in-page navigation to the relevant sections. Add a site-specific favicon and strong metadata. This is a landing page only; no authentication or database yet.
